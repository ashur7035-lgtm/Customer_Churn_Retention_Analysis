## 🎯 Business Objectives & Analytical Questions
To drive data-driven decision-making, this project aimed to answer the following business questions:

- Which customer segments (e.g., contract types, tenure groups) exhibit the highest churn rates?
- Does providing technical support significantly reduce customer attrition?
- Is there a correlation between high monthly charges and increased churn probability?
- How do different contract types (Month-to-Month vs. Annual) impact long-term customer retention?
- Does tenure length serve as a reliable predictor for churn behavior?
- Which specific high-risk customer segments should be prioritized for urgent retention campaigns?
